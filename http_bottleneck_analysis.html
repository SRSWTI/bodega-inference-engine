What changes with batched token emission

Instead of flushing one SSE frame per engine step per stream, the optimised path buffers tokens for ~5–10ms and emits in small bursts. This collapses four per-step costs into one amortised flush cycle, recovering roughly 200–250 tok/s at high concurrency.
